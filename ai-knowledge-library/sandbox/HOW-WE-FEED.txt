How we feed the talking box (sandbox)

It does not wander the live website by itself.

Deneene (Home, Library, Observatory, Holistic, Practitioners directory, Calendar)
  Answers from ai-knowledge-library/ — cards + visitor questions.
  serve.py packs the matching cards for each question.
  Today / this week comes from live TEC via pack_for_guide.py (no prices).
  Edit facts in build_library.py, then rebuild the JSON. Do not hand-edit both.

Lisa Maria (her page only)
  facts/lisa-maria.txt plus her opener. Book after two user questions.

Everyone else (sandbox pages)
  Short fact sheet in facts/{name}.txt
  That person only. Not the whole library.

When someone types, the box is only allowed to use that pack.

Always sandbox first. Live Helper PHP is a copy in live-helper/.
