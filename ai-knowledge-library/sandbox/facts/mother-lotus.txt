FROM THEIR LIVE PAGE
Mother Lotus (Maureen) is a Sacred Feminine Facilitator who creates sacred space for women to gather, reflect, and honor the cycles of nature and their own divine essence. Through Lotus Moons, Full Moon Women&#8217;s Circles, and New Moon Women&#8217;s Circles, she guides women in reconnecting with their inner wisdom and honoring the lunar cycles.
Lotus Moons — Lotus Moons gatherings honor the lunar cycles and the sacred feminine energy that flows through all of us. These ceremonies create space for women to connect with the moon&#8217;s wisdom, release what no longer serves, and set intentions aligned with their highest purpose.
The Sacred Pause (120 minutes) — The Sacred Pause offers a deeper dive into stillness and self-reflection. This extended session invites you to step away from the busyness of life and enter a sacred container where you can rest, restore, and reconnect with your authentic self. Experience the power of intentional pausing.
New Moon Women&#8217;s Circle — Join Mother Lotus for transformative New Moon Women&#8217;s Circles where women gather to set intentions, share wisdom, and support one another. These circles honor the new moon&#8217;s energy of new beginnings, planting seeds for the cycle ahead in a sacred and supportive environment.
The Wintering Within — The Wintering Within is a seasonal practice of going inward, honoring the dormant season as a time for deep rest, reflection, and inner renewal. Like nature in winter, we are invited to slow down, conserve our energy, and prepare for the rebirth that comes with spring.
