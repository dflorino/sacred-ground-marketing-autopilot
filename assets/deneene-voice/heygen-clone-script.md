# Deneene · HeyGen Instant Voice Clone

**Founder checklist:** record on iPhone → transfer to Mac → upload to HeyGen → test a short Sacred Ground line.

**Why this script is built this way:** HeyGen Instant Voice Clone learns best from about **1–2 minutes of clean, expressive speech** (30 seconds is the floor; quality beats length). Cloning tends to flatten energy, so this read deliberately mixes warm welcome, soft meditation tone, a question, and a short bright line — plus Sacred Ground words you’ll reuse on reels — so the clone sounds like *you*, not a flat narrator.

Sources: [HeyGen — How to perfect your AI voice clone](https://www.heygen.com/blog/how-to-perfect-your-ai-voice-clone) · [HeyGen voice cloning](https://www.heygen.com/tool/ai-voice-cloning) · ElevenLabs IVC guidance (phonetic/emotional variety, one speaker, quiet room).

## 1. Exact recording workflow (iPhone → Mac → HeyGen)

### Before you record (iPhone Voice Memos)

1. Open **Settings → Apps → Voice Memos → Audio Quality** (or **Settings → Voice Memos → Audio Quality** on older iOS).
2. Set **Lossless** if available (best). If not, use the highest quality option.
3. Silence notifications / put phone on Do Not Disturb.
4. Close windows; turn off fans, TV, music, dishwasher, HVAC if noisy.
5. Prefer a carpeted room or soft furnishings (closet/bedroom > bathroom/kitchen — less echo).

### How to sit / hold the phone

- Sit upright, relaxed shoulders; smile a little before you start (it shows in the voice).
- Hold the **bottom of the iPhone** (mic end) about **6 inches** from your mouth — not against fabric, not covering the mic.
- Speak at a **steady, natural volume** — a touch more expressive than everyday chat (clones flatten; don’t whisper or shout).
- One continuous take is ideal. If you stumble, pause, restart that sentence cleanly, or re-do the whole Part A/B section.

### Record

1. Open **Voice Memos → New Recording**.
2. Do a **5-second test** (say your name + “Sacred Ground”), play back — check for echo, clipping, or room noise.
3. Read **Part A** (must-have). If you have energy, continue into **Part B** without stopping.
4. Stop → rename memo: `deneene-heygen-clone-YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. Optional: record a second full take and pick the warmer one later.

### Transfer to Mac (simplest paths)

**Option A — AirDrop (preferred)**  
1. On iPhone: open the memo → **Share → AirDrop → your Mac**.  
2. On Mac: accept → file lands in Downloads (usually `.m4a`).

**Option B — Messages**  
1. Share the memo to yourself in Messages → open on Mac → Save to Downloads.

**Option C — Files / iCloud**  
1. Share → **Save to Files** → iCloud Drive → open on Mac.

No need to convert unless HeyGen rejects the file. Voice Memos **Lossless / high-quality `.m4a`** is what HeyGen’s own guidance expects from iPhone.

### What to upload to HeyGen

| Prefer | Also OK | Avoid |
|--------|---------|--------|
| `.m4a` from Voice Memos (Lossless) | `.wav`, `.mp3`, clear `.mov`/`.mp4` audio | Compressed social downloads, Zoom with other speakers, music beds |

- Target length: **~90 seconds minimum** (Part A alone); **~2–3 minutes** if you include Part B.
- One file, one speaker.

### Checklist before upload

- [ ] Quiet room — no music, TV, or chatter  
- [ ] Only **your** voice (no second speaker)  
- [ ] No heavy echo / bathroom reverb  
- [ ] Mic ~6" from mouth; no rubbing fabric  
- [ ] Natural pace; expressive, not monotone  
- [ ] Short natural pauses (don’t leave long dead air)  
- [ ] No throat-clearing / cough left in if you can trim  
- [ ] You own the rights to this voice (it’s you)  
- [ ] In HeyGen: enable **remove background noise** if offered

## 2. Read-aloud script

**How to use:** Read only the spoken lines. Follow **[bracket]** cues — do not say them out loud.  
**Timing:** Part A alone ≈ **~90 seconds**. Parts A+B ≈ **~2–3 minutes** at a natural pace (~350–400 words total).  
**If short on time:** Stop after Part A. That is enough for Instant Voice Clone.

---

### Part A — Must-have (~90 seconds)

[warm, smile in your voice]

Hi — I’m Deneene, and welcome to Sacred Ground.

[pause]

Whether you’re stepping into our shop in Arlington Heights for the first time, or you’ve been part of this community for years, I’m so glad you’re here.

[clear, grounded]

Sacred Ground is a place to breathe. A place for crystals and cards, healing and meditation, curiosity and real connection — not perfection.

[softer / meditation tone · slower]

Take a slow breath with me.  
In through the nose…  
and gently out.  
You don’t have to rush. You can just arrive.

[pause · back to warm conversational]

Have you been craving a little more stillness lately? Or looking for a reading, a class, or simply someone who truly listens?

[smile in your voice]

Come visit us. We’re here for you — body, mind, and spirit. There’s always a seat for you at Sacred Ground.

[gentle close]

Thank you for being here. I can’t wait to welcome you in person.

---

### Part B — Extra variety (keep going if you can)

[slightly brighter energy — still you, not salesy]

And if you’re planning your week — yes, we have events, practitioners, and quiet moments on the calendar. Arlington Heights is easy to find, and the shop feels like a soft landing after a full day.

[question · natural rise]

What would feel good right now — a meditation, a reading, or just browsing the shelves until something calls your name?

[short excited line · one breath of sparkle]

Oh — and when that crystal finds *you*? That’s the best part.

[soft again]

At Sacred Ground we believe your path is personal. There’s no one right way to begin. Just honest curiosity, a little courage, and a warm hello at the door.

[clear brand words · unhurried]

You can find us online at shop sacred ground dot com — that’s shopsacredground.com — or come see us in Arlington Heights.

[pause · warm, sincere]

Meditation. Welcome. Community. Sacred Ground.  
I’m Deneene — and I’m so happy you’re here.

## 3. After upload — next steps in HeyGen

1. **Create Instant Voice Clone** — AI Studio → Voices → **Create new voice** → **Instant voice clone** → upload your `.m4a` → confirm you own the voice → turn on **remove background noise** if shown.  
2. **Name it clearly** — e.g. `Deneene SG · warm 2026-08` so you never mix test clones.  
3. **Assign to your avatar** — open your Sacred Ground / Deneene avatar → set this clone as the default speaking voice.  
4. **Test with brand lines** — generate a short clip: *“Welcome to Sacred Ground in Arlington Heights.”* and *“Join us for meditation.”* Preview; regenerate any flat or weird line (TTS regenerations are free to iterate).  
5. **Lock pronunciations** — if *Sacred Ground*, *Arlington Heights*, or *shopsacredground.com* sound off, add them to the **brand glossary** with a phonetic spelling and save for future videos. Then use **Voice Director** (calm / warm) on real reel scripts.
