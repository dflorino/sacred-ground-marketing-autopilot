# HeyGen store backgrounds — shoot list

Drop **new interior / case** photos here for AI-Deneene reels (Instagram + Facebook Reels primary; TikTok/YT optional later).

Exterior storefront is already covered (`../sg-store-background.jpg` and `assets/refs/exterior-*.png`). **Today’s priority: interiors.**

## What to shoot (Founder — today)

Aim for **6–12 upright photos**. Empty of people if possible. Good natural or warm shop light.

| Priority | Subject | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Crystal / display cases** (front-on, glass clean) | Clean plate behind avatar; product texture without faces |
| 2 | **Aisles** (looking down a quiet aisle) | Depth + shop feel for 9:16 crop |
| 3 | **Logo wall / checkout glow** | Brand recognition without busy clutter |
| 4 | **Books / oracle / incense shelf** (soft focus OK) | Warm “settle in” vibe for meditation reels |
| 5 | **One wide open floor** (no customers) | Room to place avatar without covering product |

Skip for now: close-ups of faces, crowded events, messy counters, photos with the Founder in frame (avatar will be Deneene).

## How to shoot

1. **Phone upright (portrait)** — reels are **9:16**. Landscape is OK as a backup; we’ll crop.
2. Hold steady; fill the frame with the scene (no huge ceiling/floor empty zones).
3. Leave a **soft mid/lower zone** where an avatar can stand without covering the best product detail.
4. Prefer **one clear subject** per shot over a busy panorama.
5. Name files clearly, e.g.  
   `case-front-01.jpg` · `aisle-crystals-02.jpg` · `logo-wall-01.jpg`

## Formats

`.jpg` / `.jpeg` / `.png` / `.heic` (convert HEIC → JPG on Mac before upload if HeyGen rejects).
