## First-screen feeling

“This looks peaceful. This feels beautiful. I wonder what they do here.”

Not: “Whoa—where am I going?”

## Picture

Photorealistic, inspired by the real shop — warm, sophisticated, welcoming,
contemporary. A peaceful corner:

- Comfortable chair or treatment area
- One beautiful plant
- One or two crystals — not fifty
- A candle
- Perhaps a singing bowl
- Soft natural sunlight through the room

Magic is extremely subtle: sunlight slowly moving, dust in the beam, a
candle flicker, a leaf that barely moves, the faintest warm gold in the
light — readable as just beautiful light.

## Never on this hero

Chakras. Spirits. Angels. Floating tarot. Sacred geometry. Cosmic portal.
Generic mystic AI. The social Reel Engine “impossible reveal.”
