## Before any reel — five questions

If these cannot be answered clearly, improve the concept before rendering.

1. What is ordinary at the beginning?
2. What becomes impossible?
3. What is the WOW frame?
4. What emotion should the viewer experience?
5. What single thought remains after the video ends?

Every reel needs: **movement · discovery · transformation · mystery · beauty** — and one “Wait… WHAT?”

The metaphysical world has always existed directly beneath ordinary reality. For eight seconds we let them see it.

## 3-beat formula (house style — HARD)

**REALITY → IMPOSSIBLE REVEAL → THOUGHT**

| Beat | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Reality** | 0–2s | Something recognizable and almost real (crystal, card, bowl, candle, person, pendulum, book, mirror, smoke, doorway). |
| **2 · Reveal** | ~2–6s | Camera *is* how we discover the impossible: enter, pass through, follow a wave, travel inside. Smooth portal — not a hard cut. This is the WOW. |
| **3 · Thought** | last ~2s | Image breathes. **One** short line (about 3–10 words). Then tiny **SACRED GROUND**. |

Do not explain the whole metaphysical concept. Make them think afterward.

## Camera (must be alive)

No static AI image + digital zoom.

Forward travel · macro-to-micro · orbit · fly through · parallax · depth · rack focus · push / pull · vertical ascent · passing through surfaces.

## Movement / light / geometry / audio

Something always moves, organically: dust, smoke, flame, refraction, particles, energy, stars, water, pages, mist.

Cinematic light: candlelight, deep shadow, gold, moonlight, crystal refraction, volumetric, subtle violet / indigo / amber / celestial blue. **Black is useful.** Do not fill every inch.

Sacred geometry only if it *emerges* from sound, refraction, energy, water, smoke, stars, light. Never paste Flower of Life on top.

Audio later: crystal resonance, bowl, low hum, cosmic rumble, soft impact on reveal. No generic inspirational corporate music. (Kling jobs currently `generate_audio: false` until she asks for sound.)

## Text

- House type for **every** reel: **Avenir Next Medium** (not italic serif, not greeting-card script). Same face so a Sacred Ground reel is recognizable before the logo.
- One primary line. Sophisticated, cinematic. Gentle fade. No bouncing, no rainbow type, no giant TikTok captions.
- Never cover the most beautiful part of the picture.
- Branding never overpowers. Experience first; Sacred Ground second.
- Favor *What if… / Imagine… / Have you ever felt… / Some believe… / Maybe…*
- Do **not** make definitive scientific claims.
